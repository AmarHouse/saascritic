Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: August 2026

What Are Affiliate Links?

When you click on certain links on our site and make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the software vendor. This is called an "affiliate link." The price you pay is exactly the same — you don't pay anything extra. The commission comes from the vendor's marketing budget.

How We Earn Revenue

We earn revenue through:

  • Affiliate commissions: When you sign up for a product through our links, we receive a commission (typically 20-60% of your first payment, or a flat fee).
  • Sponsored content: Occasionally, we publish sponsored reviews. These are clearly labeled "Sponsored" at the top.
  • Display advertising: We show ads on some pages.

Our Editorial Independence

We never accept payment for a positive review. Our scoring system is based on real testing, not on how much a vendor pays us. A product's commission rate does NOT influence its ranking or score.

We test every product the same way — by signing up, using it for 30 days, and scoring it on our published 9-dimension methodology. If a product scores poorly, we say so — even if we earn a commission from it.

Transparency Commitment

We commit to:

  • Disclosing affiliate relationships on every page that contains affiliate links
  • Clearly labeling sponsored content
  • Never selling placement in our rankings
  • Updating our reviews when products change significantly
  • Responding to corrections and feedback

FTC Compliance

This disclosure is provided in accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255). We disclose our affiliate relationships clearly and conspicuously on every page containing affiliate links.

Questions?

If you have any questions about our affiliate relationships or editorial process, please contact us at hello@site.com.
