Every product is scored on 9 dimensions. Each dimension has a weight based on importance to most users.

Our Testing Process

Every product goes through the same 4-step process.

1 Sign Up & Onboarding

  • Create a real account (free trial or paid)
  • Complete onboarding process
  • Measure time-to-value
  • Evaluate first impressions

2 Build Real Workflows

  • Create email campaigns
  • Set up automations
  • Build landing pages
  • Test integrations

3 30-Day Trial

  • Use product daily for 30 days
  • Test deliverability and speed
  • Contact support with questions
  • Monitor uptime and reliability

4 Score & Publish

  • Score on 9 dimensions
  • Capture screenshots
  • Document pros and cons
  • Compare against alternatives
