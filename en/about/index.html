About SaaS Review Portal

We help entrepreneurs choose the right SaaS tools through expert testing and transparent scoring.

Our Mission

The SaaS market is crowded with choices. Every tool claims to be the best. We cut through the noise by actually testing every product we recommend — for 30 days, using real workflows, with no bias toward any vendor.

Our goal is simple: help you make a confident, informed purchase decision — and save you time and money in the process.

How We're Different

🧪 We Actually Test

We sign up for every product, build real campaigns, and use them for 30+ days. No screenshots from other sites, no recycled press releases.

📊 Transparent Scoring

Our 9-dimension scoring system is published. You can see exactly how we calculate each score — no black boxes.

🔍 Real Screenshots

We capture actual screenshots from our test accounts. You see exactly what the product looks like — not marketing mockups.

💰 Affiliate Transparency

Our Testing Process

1

Sign Up & Onboarding

We create a real account using the same process a new user would. We measure how long it takes to get started and how intuitive the onboarding is.

2

Build Real Workflows

We build email campaigns, automations, landing pages, and integrations — the same things you'd build with the product.

3

Test for 30 Days

We use the product daily for 30 days. This includes sending emails, testing deliverability, measuring speed, and evaluating support responsiveness.

4

Score & Document

We score on 9 dimensions using our published methodology. We capture screenshots, document pros/cons, and compare against alternatives.

Our Scoring System

We score every product on 9 dimensions. Each dimension has a different weight based on importance to most users.

Dimension Weight What We Measure
Features 20% Core functionality, depth, breadth
Ease of Use 15% Learning curve, interface design, documentation
Pricing 15% Value for money, free tier, plan flexibility
Support 10% Response time, channel options, quality
Reliability 10% Uptime, speed, bug frequency
Integrations 10% Number, quality, and ease of setup
Scalability 10% Handles growth, pricing at scale
Community 5% Active forums, tutorials, resources
Innovation 5% Regular updates, new features, roadmap
