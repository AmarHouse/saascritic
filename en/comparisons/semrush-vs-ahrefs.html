Who Should Choose Semrush?

  • Businesses that need an all-in-one marketing toolkit
  • Teams that do both SEO and PPC advertising
  • Content marketers who need topic research and content optimization
  • Agencies that need competitor analysis and reporting
  • Businesses with local SEO needs

Who Should Choose Ahrefs?

  • SEO professionals who focus on link building
  • Teams that need the best backlink data available
  • Businesses that prefer a simpler, more focused interface
  • Technical SEO specialists who need site audit features
  • Budget-conscious teams that want solid SEO tools
