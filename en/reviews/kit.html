What is Kit?

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) is an email marketing platform built specifically for content creators. Unlike traditional email marketing tools, Kit is designed for bloggers, podcasters, YouTubers, and other creators who want to build relationships with their audience through email.

Who is Kit Best For?

  • Bloggers who want to grow their email list
  • Podcasters who want to engage listeners
  • YouTubers who want to connect with subscribers
  • Authors who want to build a readership
  • Coaches who want simple email automation

Key Features

  • Email Designer: Clean, text-focused email templates
  • Automation: Visual automation builder
  • Landing Pages: Simple, high-converting pages
  • Commerce: Sell digital products directly
  • Creator Network: Cross-promote with other creators
  • Integrations: WordPress, Zapier, and more
