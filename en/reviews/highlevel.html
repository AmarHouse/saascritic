What is HighLevel?

HighLevel (also known as GoHighLevel) is an all-in-one marketing platform designed for marketing agencies. It combines CRM, email marketing, funnels, scheduling, and more into a single subscription.

The platform is white-label ready, meaning agencies can rebrand it and offer it to their clients as their own platform.
