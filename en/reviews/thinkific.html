What is Thinkific?

Thinkific is a course creation platform that lets you build, market, and sell online courses, memberships, and digital products. It's designed for entrepreneurs, educators, and businesses who want to monetize their expertise without needing technical skills.

Who is Thinkific Best For?

  • Course creators who want to sell pre-recorded or live courses
  • Coaches who want to package their knowledge into sellable courses
  • Educators who need a learning management system (LMS)
  • Businesses that want to train employees or customers

Key Features

  • Course Builder: Drag-and-drop editor with multimedia support
  • Website Builder: Customizable sales pages and course websites
  • Marketing Tools: Coupons, affiliates, email integration
  • Community: Built-in student community features
  • Analytics: Student progress tracking and revenue reports
  • Integrations: Zapier, Mailchimp, ConvertKit, and more
