What is Semrush?

Semrush is an all-in-one marketing toolkit used by over 10 million marketing professionals. It provides tools for SEO, PPC, content marketing, social media, and competitive research.

It's the industry standard for SEO professionals and agencies. If you're serious about organic search, Semrush is likely the best tool available.
