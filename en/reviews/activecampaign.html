ActiveCampaign Review

The email marketing platform with 900+ integrations and visual automation builder

8.5 A Overall Score

TL;DR — Who Is ActiveCampaign For?

ActiveCampaign is best for businesses that need advanced email marketing automation without paying enterprise prices. It combines email marketing, marketing automation, and CRM in one platform.

Best for: Growing businesses needing automation Skip if: You only need basic email newsletters

With 900+ integrations and a visual automation builder, ActiveCampaign is a solid choice for small to mid-size businesses that want to automate their marketing without breaking the bank.

What We Like

  • Visual automation builder with drag-and-drop
  • 900+ integrations (more than most competitors)
  • Conditional content in emails
  • Lead scoring and segmentation
  • Site tracking and event tracking
  • Generous free trial (14 days)

What Could Be Better

  • Steep learning curve for beginners
  • Pricing increases quickly with contacts
  • Free plan discontinued
  • Reporting could be more intuitive
  • Phone support only on higher plans

Key Features

Marketing Automation

Visual builder with drag-and-drop automation workflows. Create complex sequences based on user behavior, tags, and custom fields.

Email Marketing

Beautiful email templates with conditional content. Send personalized emails based on subscriber behavior and preferences.

CRM Integration

Built-in CRM for managing contacts and deals. Track interactions and automate follow-ups based on deal stages.

900+ Integrations

Connect with your favorite tools including Shopify, WordPress, Zapier, and more. Native integrations with popular platforms.

Lead Scoring

Score leads based on behavior and demographics. Automatically qualify leads and trigger actions based on scores.

Site Tracking

Track visitor behavior on your website. See which pages contacts visit and automate responses based on activity.
