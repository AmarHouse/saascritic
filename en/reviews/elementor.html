Elementor Review

The most popular WordPress page builder with drag-and-drop design

9.1 A+ Overall Score

TL;DR — Who Is Elementor For?

Elementor is best for anyone building WordPress websites who wants design freedom without writing code. It's the most popular page builder with over 12 million active installs.

Best for: WordPress users who want visual design control Skip if: You don't use WordPress (consider Webflow)

Elementor gives you a live drag-and-drop editor with 100+ widgets and 300+ templates. You can build any page layout without touching a line of code.

What We Like

  • Live drag-and-drop editor (what you see is what you get)
  • 100+ widgets for any content type
  • 300+ professionally designed templates
  • Responsive design controls
  • WooCommerce builder for e-commerce
  • Free version available with core features

What Could Be Better

  • Can slow down WordPress if overused
  • Premium widgets locked behind paid plans
  • Limited design options compared to custom code
  • Some templates look generic
  • No built-in A/B testing

Key Features

Drag & Drop Editor

Build pages visually with a live editor. See changes in real-time as you design. No coding required.

100+ Widgets

Add any content type — headings, images, videos, forms, sliders, galleries, testimonials, and more.

300+ Templates

Start with professionally designed templates. Customize colors, fonts, and content to match your brand.

Responsive Design

Control how your pages look on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Adjust spacing, fonts, and layouts per device.

WooCommerce Builder

Build custom product pages, cart pages, and checkout flows. Design your entire WooCommerce store.

Theme Builder

Design your entire WordPress theme visually — headers, footers, single posts, archives, and 404 pages.
