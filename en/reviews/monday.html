monday.com Review

The work OS that turns any workflow into a powerful app

8.9 A Overall Score

TL;DR — Who Is monday.com For?

monday.com is best for teams that need customizable project management with visual dashboards and automations. It's a "work OS" that can handle projects, CRM, development, and more.

Best for: Teams needing visual, flexible workflows Skip if: You need complex automation (use ActiveCampaign)

monday.com shines with its visual interface and flexibility. You can create custom workflows for almost anything, from project management to sales pipelines to content calendars.

What We Like

  • Highly visual and intuitive interface
  • Flexible — works for almost any workflow
  • Built-in automations and integrations
  • Multiple views (Kanban, Gantt, Calendar, etc.)
  • Great mobile apps
  • Free plan for up to 2 seats

What Could Be Better

  • Pricing increases quickly with seats
  • Can feel overwhelming for simple projects
  • Automations limited on lower plans
  • No built-in time tracking on all plans
  • Reporting requires higher-tier plans

Key Features

Work OS

Build custom workflows for any use case — project management, CRM, development, marketing, and more. No code required.

Automations

Create rules to automate repetitive tasks. Trigger actions based on status changes, dates, or custom conditions.

Multiple Views

Switch between Kanban, Gantt, Calendar, Timeline, and Table views. Visualize your work the way you want.

Dashboards

Build custom dashboards with widgets. Track progress, budgets, and KPIs across multiple boards.

Integrations

Connect with 200+ tools including Slack, Google Drive, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams.

Templates

Choose from 200+ pre-built templates for different industries and use cases. Get started in minutes.
